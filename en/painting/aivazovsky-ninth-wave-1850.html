Painting record · 1850

The Ninth Wave

欧洲 · 油画（布面）

The Ninth Wave shows dawn after a storm. The sea is still torn by huge waves; six survivors cling to a piece of wreckage on the crest; the sky moves from deep blue to orange-gold as sunlight breaks through cloud. The central wave rises like a transparent wall, almost swallowing the figures.

Aivazovsky had not witnessed this particular storm, yet he paints the transparency, foam and refraction of water with extraordinary conviction. He produced thousands of seascapes; this is the most quoted: nature's power and human smallness in one breath.
