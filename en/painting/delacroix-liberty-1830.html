Painting record · 1830

Liberty Leading the People

欧洲 · 油画（布面）

Liberty Leading the People turns the barricades of the July Revolution of 1830 into myth. Liberty stands half-naked at the centre, the tricolour raised in her right hand, a musket in her left, striding over bodies. Around her are a bourgeois in a top hat, a youth with pistols, a worker kneeling on the ground; Paris rooftops and smoke behind.

Delacroix paints the revolution not as an orderly demonstration but as a chaotic, bloody advance. The tricolour is the brightest colour in the picture, a lighthouse drawing the crowd towards it. The painting later became one of the symbols of the French Republic.
