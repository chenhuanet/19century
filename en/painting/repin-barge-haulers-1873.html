Painting record · 1870–1873

Barge Haulers on the Volga

欧洲 · 油画（布面）

Barge Haulers on the Volga shows a team of eleven men hauling a rope along the river, bodies leaning forward, feet sunk in sand. At the front an old man stares ahead; behind him a younger man tries to lift his head. The broad river, a distant boat and a hot sky lie behind them.

As a student Repin travelled the Volga and began the picture after seeing the haulers. He turns Russian poverty and endurance into a physical epic: no heroes, only people joined by a rope.
