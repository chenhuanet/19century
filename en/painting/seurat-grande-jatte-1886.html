A Sunday on La Grande Jatte

欧洲 · 油画（布面）

A Sunday on La Grande Jatte shows a park beside the Seine: people sit on grass, hold parasols, walk dogs, row boats, all like silhouettes paused mid-motion. The surface is built from countless small dots of colour; from a distance they read as unified green and blue, up close as grains of orange, violet and yellow. Figures are reduced to vertical and horizontal blocks, even shadows measured as if by ruler.

Seurat brings colour science, optical theory and classical composition into one picture. It extends Impressionism's interest in light and contradicts its "instant": everything here is still and calculable, a specimen of modern city life.
