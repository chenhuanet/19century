The Starry Night

欧洲 · 油画（布面）

The Starry Night was painted at the asylum in Saint-Rémy. The sky becomes a swirling vortex, stars burning like fireballs, the moon rising at the right; a cypress in the foreground rises like a black flame; below lies a quiet village, its church spire pointing into the distance.

Van Gogh did not paint this sky from memory alone; he mixed the view from his window with imagination and inner turbulence. The thick, visible strokes give the picture both motion and stasis: the world turns, while the viewer is fixed at the window.
